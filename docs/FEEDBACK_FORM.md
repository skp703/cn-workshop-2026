# Workshop feedback instrument

Participant form:
<https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSeavvSSEWlNcENVxCJZW9g22rztcJpi2Cd6ba6smZ9JX9toSA/viewform>

The form is anonymous and does not collect email addresses. Questions 1–8 are
required; Questions 9–11 are optional.

## Questions

1. **Which participant-directed investigation did you work on most?**
   - CN equation and runoff response
   - Spatial CN for a watershed
   - Land-cover change and design runoff
   - Event-derived CN and antecedent state
   - I worked across more than one investigation

2. **The Curve Number theory section clarified the governing equations and
   assumptions.** Linear scale 1–5, Strongly disagree to Strongly agree.

3. **The Earth Engine introduction clarified how watershed spatial data can be
   explored and summarized.** Linear scale 1–5, Strongly disagree to Strongly
   agree.

4. **The participant-directed notebook period provided an effective balance of
   guidance and analytical choice.** Linear scale 1–5, Strongly disagree to
   Strongly agree.

5. **I can identify the data sources and assumptions that should accompany a
   reported Curve Number result.** Linear scale 1–5, Strongly disagree to
   Strongly agree.

6. **The workshop content is relevant to my professional, teaching, or research
   activities.** Linear scale 1–5, Strongly disagree to Strongly agree.

7. **The allocation of time among theory, Earth Engine, investigation, and
   discussion was appropriate.** Linear scale 1–5, Strongly disagree to
   Strongly agree.

8. **Which topics should receive more time in a future workshop? Select all
   that apply.**
   - Curve Number theory
   - Google Earth Engine fundamentals
   - Watershed delineation
   - Spatial Curve Number estimation
   - Land-cover change and uncertainty
   - Event-derived Curve Numbers and calibration
   - Participant discussion and report-outs

9. **What was the most valuable part of the workshop?** Paragraph response.

10. **What is one specific change that would improve the workshop?** Paragraph
    response.

11. **Please share any additional comments or topics you would like addressed
    in a future offering.** Paragraph response.

## Suggested analysis

- Summarize rating distributions with medians and response counts rather than
  reporting only means.
- Stratify the notebook-period rating by the selected investigation when the
  response count permits.
- Review open responses using a small thematic codebook: conceptual content,
  Earth Engine, notebooks, pacing, logistics, and requested follow-up.
- Retain the number of responses with every summary.
